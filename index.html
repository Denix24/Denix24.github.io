Capstone Project — HVALIDATE

Presenteeism Clinical Validation Dashboard

HVALIDATE is a healthcare analytics dashboard designed to evaluate the impact of presenteeism in workplace environments while preserving employee privacy. The system processes survey data, validates responses, and generates cohort-level insights that help organizations understand productivity loss trends and support evidence-based decision-making.

My Role

I contributed to the design of the analytics pipeline, dashboard logic, and privacy-preserving reporting framework. My work focused on ensuring that raw survey responses could be validated, transformed, and presented as meaningful cohort-level metrics.

Key Features
  • Data validation and response consistency checks
  • Severity stage metrics showing distribution across risk levels
  • Cohort-based reporting to protect individual privacy
  • Aggregated analytics outputs for leadership insights
  • Cost impact estimation of productivity loss
Privacy and Security Approach
  • Use of synthetic datasets for demonstration purposes
  • Cohort-level outputs rather than individual-level reporting
  • Minimum group-size thresholds to maintain anonymity
  • Planned IAM-based access control for secure online deployment
Skills Demonstrated

Python, Streamlit, data validation, dashboard design, cohort analytics, privacy-by-design, cloud deployment planning

What I Learned

This project strengthened my ability to balance analytics value with privacy protection. It also deepened my understanding of how dashboards, data validation, and governance principles must work together to support secure and meaningful organizational intelligence.
