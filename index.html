Capstone Project — HVALIDATE

Presenteeism Clinical Validation Dashboard

HVALIDATE is a healthcare analytics dashboard designed to evaluate the impact of presenteeism in workplace environments while preserving employee privacy. The system processes survey responses, validates inputs, and generates cohort-level insights that help organizations understand productivity loss trends and support evidence-based decision-making.

Project Description

The dashboard focuses on translating survey-based health and productivity indicators into meaningful validation metrics such as severity stages, cohort summaries, and estimated cost impact. It is designed to support leadership decisions while ensuring that sensitive employee information is not exposed.

My Role

I contributed to the design of the analytics pipeline, validation logic, dashboard interpretation, and privacy-preserving reporting framework. My work focused on ensuring that raw survey responses could be transformed into reliable cohort-level metrics for stakeholder use.

Key Features
  • Data validation and response consistency checks
  • Severity stage metrics showing workforce risk distribution
  • Cohort-based reporting to protect individual privacy
  • Aggregated analytics outputs for leadership insights
  • Cost impact estimation of productivity loss
Skills Demonstrated

Python, Streamlit, data validation, dashboard design, cohort analytics, privacy-by-design, cloud deployment planning

What I Learned

This project strengthened my understanding of how analytics systems must balance actionable insight with privacy protection. It also deepened my experience in designing dashboards that integrate validation logic, governance principles, and stakeholder-focused reporting.
